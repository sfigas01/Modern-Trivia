Rating guide
  • Correct and one answer are pass/fail. A "No" on either means the verdict can't be Keep. Use Unsure rather than guessing.
  • Fun: can the room reason toward it? Is the reveal satisfying? Would this audience recognize or enjoy it? Trick wording, giveaways and textbook definitions lower it.
  • Difficulty: Easy means most players get it, Medium about half, Hard few but still fair.
  • Fits: Canadian-friendly or globally relevant, not US-default, on theme, right for a mixed adult group.
  • Fix means a good fact with wording, accepted answers or a difficulty label that needs work.
  • Try to answer before you reveal the answer. Questions come from several sources, mixed in random order.
